# Privacy policy

**Twenty-One (廿一)**  
Effective 12 November 2026

Twenty-One is a 21-day self-discipline check-in. You pick a time. Once a day, inside a three-hour window, you light a star. This policy is the one to host for the App Store and for Google Play.

## Data we collect

We do not collect data. There is no account, no server for your check-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

The following stays on your device:

- the check-in time you chose
- the current cycle, finished constellations, and interrupted cycles
- whether reminders are on
- a local note that Pro is unlocked, once a Pro product exists

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the phone. They are not sent from a server we operate.

Export writes a JSON file only when you tap Export. Import reads a file you choose. Sharing the day-21 card uses the system share sheet and sends the image only to the app you pick.

## Purchases

This version does not sell anything. The app includes a billing client so a one-time Pro unlock can be added later. Until product identifiers are filled in, that client is not started.

When a purchase is offered, Apple or Google processes the payment. We do not receive your card number, and we do not run a receipt server. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the version that changes it is submitted.

## Backups

Android Auto Backup is turned off. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple may include this app’s local storage in the backup you control. We do not receive that backup.

## Children

Twenty-One is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## 隐私政策

廿一（Twenty-One）是一个 21 天自律打卡应用。我们不收集数据。没有账号，没有服务器，没有统计，没有广告，没有追踪。

打卡时刻、当前周期、完成的星座和中断记录只保存在你的设备上。提醒由系统在本机安排，不是我们的服务器推送。只有你点击导出时才会生成备份文件。分享第 21 天的卡片时，图片只发给你在系统分享单里选中的应用。

这个版本不销售任何内容。应用里带有商店购买组件，以便日后加入一次性的 Pro 解锁。在填入商品编号之前，该组件不会启动。一旦开放购买，付款由 Apple 或 Google 处理，我们不会收到你的支付卡信息，也不会自建收据服务器。

Android 自动备份已关闭。如果你开启了 iCloud 设备备份，Apple 可能会把本应用的本地数据放进由你控制的备份里，我们不会收到那份备份。

本应用不以 13 岁以下儿童为对象。
