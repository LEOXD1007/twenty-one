## Description

```
Twenty-one days. Twenty-one stars.

Pick a time. Each day, a three-hour window opens at that moment. Press and hold to light one star. You cannot light it early. If the window closes first, the cycle ends and you start again from day one. The days you kept are still yours.

Finish all twenty-one and the stars join into a constellation. Where each star sits depends on when, inside the window, you came back. No two skies are the same. On the last night the app gives you a card you can keep or share.

Star map holds every finished constellation and every interrupted cycle. Records stay on this device. You can export them, and import them back. Reminders are scheduled on the phone for when the window opens and for half an hour before it closes.

A reset you can see.
```
