Twenty-One

A 21-day self-discipline check-in. Twenty-one days, twenty-one stars.

How a day works

Choose a time. Each day, a three-hour window opens at that time. Press and hold the center of the sky to light one star. You cannot check in early. If the window closes before you do, the cycle ends and the next one starts at day 1. The days you kept are saved in Star map.

Your records

Records stay on this device. Export writes a JSON backup. Import replaces what is on the device with that file. There is no account to sign in to.

Reminders

Turn on Window reminder in Star map. The phone schedules a notification when the window opens, and another 30 minutes before it closes. iOS will ask for notification permission. Android will too, on Android 13 and later.

使用说明

选定一个时刻。每天在这个时刻起的三小时内，长按星空中央点亮一颗星。不能提前。窗口结束前没有打卡，这一轮就结束，下一轮从第 1 天开始。已经坚持的天数保存在星图里。

记录只在这台设备上。导出会生成 JSON 备份，导入会用备份覆盖这台设备上的记录。没有账号。

在星图里打开开窗提醒。系统会在窗口开启时提醒一次，并在关闭前 30 分钟再提醒一次。
