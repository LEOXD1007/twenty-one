# Age rating

The app shows a night sky, a count of days, and a card at day 21. It has no user-to-user contact, no web browser, no ads, and no sexual, violent, or gambling content. Store text should stay on “21-day self-discipline” and “habit reset”.

## Apple

In App Store Connect, Age Rating, answer **None** / **No** for every frequency and for every yes/no feature:

- Cartoon or fantasy violence: None
- Realistic violence: None
- Prolonged graphic or sadistic realistic violence: None
- Profanity or crude humor: None
- Mature or suggestive themes: None
- Horror or fear themes: None
- Medical or treatment information: None
- Alcohol, tobacco, or drug use or references: None
- Simulated gambling: None
- Sexual content or nudity: None
- Graphic sexual content and nudity: None
- Contests: None
- Unrestricted web access: No
- Gambling and contests: No
- User-generated content that other users see: No. The share sheet is the system sheet, not an in-app social feed.
- Messaging, chat, or advertising: No

Expected result: **4+**.

If the questionnaire has a newer item that is not in this list, the matching answer is still None or No. Do not describe the app as sexual in the review notes. A reviewer who only sees the binary and the listing should find a habit timer.

## Google Play

IARC / content rating:

- Violence: no
- Sexuality: no
- Language: no profanity
- Controlled substances: no
- Does the app share user location with other users? No
- Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content? No
- Does the app share personal information? No
- Digital purchases: **No** for this build, because nothing is for sale. Change this to Yes in the same release that sets a product id.
- Does the app contain ads? No

Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0, or the equivalent low band.

Target audience: not designed for children. Select ages 18 and older as the target if the console asks for a single group. The content itself is suitable for a low rating. Choosing 18+ keeps the app out of Designed for Families, which is what you want. Do not check the box that says the app is a children’s app.
